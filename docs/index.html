    Questions

    Common questions

    Do I have to give it my password?

    No. There is no yTunes account, and you never type a password into it. It works inside the YouTube Music tab you are already signed in to.

    Does it play the music itself?

    No. YouTube Music plays everything, exactly as it does now. yTunes only changes what you see and passes your play, pause and skip clicks along.

    Do I need to pay for Premium?

    Whatever your account can play now, it can play with yTunes. Nothing is unlocked or skipped, so ads and limits work just as they did before.

    How do I get the normal site back?

    Click the yTunes button in your browser toolbar and switch it off, or open its settings and press “Original YouTube Music”. The page reloads back to normal, and it stays that way until you turn it on again.

    Does it work on my phone?

    No. Phone and tablet browsers cannot use add-ons like this one, so you need Chrome or Firefox on a desktop or laptop. Safari is not supported yet either.

    Is this made by Apple or Google?

    No. One person makes it, and it has nothing to do with either company. It does not use any of their pictures, logos or type.

    Set it up by hand instead

    Download the zip file and unzip it. Then:

    Chrome, Edge, Brave, Opera, Vivaldi or Arc: go to chrome://extensions, turn on “Developer mode” in the corner, click “Load unpacked” and pick the unzipped folder.

    Firefox: go to about:debugging, click “This Firefox”, then “Load Temporary Add-on” and pick any file inside the unzipped folder. Firefox forgets it when you close the browser.

    What you need

    Chrome or Firefox on a desktop or laptop. Phones and tablets cannot run it.
