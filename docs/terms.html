What Mixtunes is

Mixtunes is an independent browser extension: a classic iTunes theme for Spotify, YouTube Music, SoundCloud and Apple Music. YouTube Music works today; the other hosts are under development. It is a fan-made homage to mid-2000s music software and is provided for personal, non-commercial use.

Your use of third-party services

Mixtunes operates inside the tab of the music service you already use and does not bypass any login, payment, or access controls. Your use of YouTube Music, Spotify, Apple Music, SoundCloud, or any other service remains governed by that provider’s own terms of service. Mixtunes reads and controls the player page only to render its theme.
